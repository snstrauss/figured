# Personal Growth

## Figured

**Base list**:
The static starting collection of categories and symptom entries used when creating a profile. Personal edits do not alter this collection.

**Profile**:
A named, independent workspace created from the base list. Its categories, entries, assessments, personal notes, ordering, and archives belong to that profile alone.

**Log out**:
Leave the selected profile and return to the profile chooser. This does not involve account authentication.

**Symptom entry**:
A named item from the working list, with a general description and space for personal experience. An entry belongs to exactly one category.

**Category**:
A named group of symptom entries with its own color. Entries display the color of their current category.

**Impact rating**:
The user's current assessment of how strongly an entry affects them, from 1 (not an issue) to 5 (very strong).

**Not rated**:
An entry without an assessment. This is distinct from a rating of 1.

**General description**:
An editable explanation of what an entry describes, separate from the user's own experience.

**Personal experience**:
The user's notes and examples of how an entry relates to their life.

**Manual order**:
The current arrangement of entries within a category. Dragging or sorting can replace this arrangement; earlier arrangements are not retained separately.

**Archived entry**:
An entry hidden from the active list while retaining its category, position, rating, and text. Restoring it makes it visible again; archiving and restoring do not themselves change its position.

**Archive**:
A separate branch at the bottom of the tree, containing archived entries grouped by their categories. It appears only when archived entries exist.
